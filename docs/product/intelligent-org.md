# The Intelligent Organization

---

## What it is

An organization is intelligent when it remembers what it is for, notices what happened, and puts
the next action in front of the right person — without that person having to ask.

The AI watches the space and drafts the work. People decide. The AI never decides money, who is
in, or what the organization believes.

Memory is the asset. The model is a rented interpreter. Open the app and you should know what
matters and what to do next.

Most of the app is **people talking to each other** — 1:1 and groups — plus the org agent, in
one place (the same idea as [Buzz](https://buzz.xyz/): your people, your agents, your project).
Those threads are not a side feature. They are how the organization hears what is happening.
Every member chat is fed to the org. That is the main input the AI uses to draft tickets and
mandates.

## Who is who

| Who                    | What they are                                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **New person**         | Signed in for the first time. No profile yet.                                 |
| **Potential member**   | Outside a space. Often looking for open work they could take.                 |
| **Member**             | Joined. No job until they accept a mandate or a ticket.                       |
| **DRI**                | Accepted a mandate (holds the job and the pot) and/or a ticket (does the work). |
| **Shaper**             | What the org believes. Who else is a Shaper. Which mandates exist. How big each pot is. |
| **Investor**           | Put money or reputation in. Sees honestly. Does not run the work.             |
| **Founder**            | Sets the space up once. Then they are a member — usually also a Shaper.       |

A DRI is a relationship, not a job title. You can be DRI of the stall and of one ticket on it.
One person can be a member, a DRI, a Shaper, and an investor at once.

Joining does not make you a DRI. You are a member until you are offered a mandate or a ticket
and you say yes.

## Mandate and ticket

A **mandate** is the job you hold — with a pot, until a review.  
A **ticket** is one piece of work you finish under that job.

```
Stall (mandate — Sam — pot 1,200 — review 1 June)
  ├─ Find two Saturday covers     ← ticket
  └─ Write the Saturday setup     ← ticket
```

Someone accepts a mandate. They are the mandate DRI.

Someone accepts a ticket under it. They are the ticket DRI. They do it. They mark it done.

If no mandate covers the need, it does not land on a random DRI. It goes to Shapers as a new
mandate.

Tickets can nest under other tickets. They still spend from the **mandate** pot. A smaller
mandate is just a mandate carved from a parent pot — same object, not a special type.

## How work is created

Work shows up because people are talking — to each other, or to the org agent. There is no
“new ticket” or “carve mandate” button.

**People chat is the feed.** 1:1 and group threads in the space, plus mail and call
transcripts, are read by the org as they happen. When something needs doing, the AI drafts a
card — ticket if a mandate already covers it, mandate if nothing does. Later mentions of the
same gap attach to the same card. Members do not file work for the org to notice it. They just
talk.

**Talk to the agent** when you want to be explicit: start a space, say you need a smaller job
under Stall, spend from the pot. Same chat surface as people. The AI decides whether that is a
mandate or a ticket. Cards appear in the side. You confirm.

The AI drafts. A person still confirms before it is offered, and the named person still accepts.
Nobody is assigned.

## Journeys

**First login.** Chat. The AI asks whether they want to **join an org** or **create one** — this
can be the first question. Then it asks who they are. They can drop social links or just
usernames, and any other context about themselves. Cards fill a profile in the side. They
confirm. That is how the profile is created. No form.

- **Create.** Same chat continues into starting the space (below).
- **Join.** The AI asks what kind of org they want (purpose, place, the work they like). Then
  it shows **open jobs** matched to their profile and those answers. They pick a job — that
  opens that space’s public page with that job pinned. Join or request as usual.

**Start the space.** Conversation, not a form. The AI asks what this is for, who is here, what
must happen in 90 days, and how money works. Cards fill as they talk. The founder confirms
memory, first Shapers, first mandates, and who can join. Then founder is done.

**Join a space.** Public page: what this is, what it has done, what is still open. Join, or
request if entry is gated. One Shaper accept is enough. They land on the space, not on a work
queue. If they arrived from a matched job, that job is pinned as “you said you were
interested” — still not an assignment.

**Talk in the org.** Members open 1:1 or group chats and work in the open. The org reads those
threads. A grower asking “who signs the hall licence?” in the Saturday group is enough — the
AI drafts from that. Nobody copies it into a form.

**A need appears.** It came from a people thread, mail, a call, or someone telling the agent.
The AI drafts a ticket or a mandate. If it is a mandate, Shapers see the card, amend if they
want, and it is offered. If it is a ticket, it sits under the mandate that covers it. The
person named sees an offer and accepts or declines. Decline sends it back.

**Do a ticket.** The ticket DRI does the work and marks done. Decline is _not my thing_ or _no
room right now_.

**Nothing owns this.** Need with no mandate → one card on the Shaper home. Offer, or leave it
open so the public page can say the job needs someone.

**Put it down.** Ticket DRI stops — the ticket goes back under the mandate. Mandate DRI resigns
— tickets freeze, pot freezes, card returns to Shapers. They do not pick their successor.

**Review.** On the review date the Shaper sees what was held, what was spent, what happened.
They refill, resize, close, or offer it again — in the same chat.

## Payments

One pot per mandate. Shapers fill it. They do not sign every payment.

Money leaves three ways:

| Tap              | What                         | Pays when                                          |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Standing pay** | Named person, amount / month | On the cadence, until review. No extra approve.    |
| **Ticket pay**   | Bounty on one ticket         | When that ticket is marked done.                   |
| **Expense**      | Hall, ice, fridge, tools     | When the mandate DRI spends it.                    |

All three come from the same pot. When the mandate ends, the taps stop. Never pay a person who
has no mandate.

**Flows**

1. **Fill.** Shaper puts money in the pot (at create, or later). That is the Shaper money
   decision.
2. **Standing.** Payday hits. Money moves. Nobody taps.
3. **Ticket.** Ticket DRI marks done. Bounty pays from the pot. Mandate DRI does not re-approve.
4. **Expense.** Mandate DRI tells the AI (or confirms a draft) — amount, to whom, why. Money
   moves.
5. **Cosign** (only if the pot has one). Expense or ticket pay sits as *waiting for release*.
   The named person releases or refuses. Standing pay already sat on the mandate — it just runs.

If the spend does not fit any pot, that is a Shaper decision: fill or resize a pot. It is not a
payment the DRI can invent.

## Screens

**Chats.** The main surface. 1:1 and group threads with people, and threads with the org
agent. All of it is fed to the org. First login (profile, join vs create) is an agent thread.
Creating work or spending is an agent thread. Day to day is people.

**Find work.** After they said join: open jobs matched to their profile and what they asked
for. They pick one and land on that space.

**Public space.** Brief, what is open, join or request.

**The space** (no role yet). Purpose, who is here, open jobs. Any offer they have. No queue.

**What needs a Shaper?** One list: mandates to confirm, join requests, memory to confirm, pots
to fill, cosigns waiting, review dates. Empty says *Nothing needs a Shaper*.

**Offer.** One screen for both kinds. Mandate: the job, the pot, the three taps, the review
date. Ticket: the draft, why them, the bounty if any. Accept / decline.

**What I hold** (mandate DRI). Pot left. Standing pay still to land. Tickets waiting. Expenses
already spent. Chat to add work or spend. Put the mandate down.

**What needs me?** (ticket DRI). One card, or *Nothing needs you*. Open it: start, done, or
decline.

**Ticket.** Draft already written. Why this, why you. Done pays if there is a bounty.

**Pot.** Remaining / total, the split, the ledger. Child mandates show under the parent. Cosign
shows *waiting*.

**Overview** (funder, or anyone looking in). What we said, what we did, the pots, what changed.
No work buttons.

Homes do not mix. A Shaper who is also a DRI switches. Stall tickets never appear on the Shaper
list. A Shaper card never appears on *What needs me?*
